Design decisions

  • 01 Warm tone matches the product. The sherpa bed is the same warm honey-cream. The CTA echoes the product's texture — before the customer even sees it up close, the button is already priming the association.
  • 02 Works on both dark and light backgrounds. The current white-outline button disappears on light sections (buy block, reviews, final CTA). Butter is visible everywhere without a second button variant.
  • 03 Bottom border lip = tactile click affordance. 2.5px border-bottom vs 1.5px everywhere else. Subconsciously reads as a physical object you press down. The :active state collapses it to 1.5px and adds translateY(1px) so it genuinely feels like pressing a button.
  • 04 Arrow shifts right on hover. The → translates 4px right with a spring overshoot (cubic-bezier 0.34,1.56). Directional energy — points toward the cart without screaming urgency.
  • 05 Simple → instead of split-tail SVG. On the outlined ghost button, the Mily split-tail arrow adds geometric weight that balances a thin border. On a filled button the split-tail competes with the fill. A plain → reads cleaner and feels friendlier.
  • 06 Zero new CSS variables. --butter, --butter-border, --butter-press, --butter-text — all four are additive. The text token reuses existing --color-brown. Drop-in replacement for .btn--primary-fill.
